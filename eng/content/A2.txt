# 1 | Büyük haber | Big News
Last week, Mr Brown gave us some big news. There is a summer English course in London for students. It is four weeks long, and the university pays for half of the price. I was very excited. I talked to my parents in the evening. My father said: "It is a great chance. You should go." I decided to apply.

# 2 | Başvuru formu | The Application Form
Yesterday I filled in the application form. It asked for my name, my address, my date of birth and my phone number. I also wrote a short letter. In the letter, I explained why I wanted to go to London. I wrote about my love for engineering and my dream of working abroad. Then I sent the form by email.

# 3 | Bekleyiş | Waiting
For two weeks, I waited for an answer. Every morning I checked my email. Nothing! I started to worry. Can said: "Do not worry. They received many forms, so they need time." He was right. On a Tuesday afternoon, an email arrived: "Dear Deniz, we are happy to tell you that you have a place on the course." I shouted with joy.

# 4 | Pasaport ve vize | Passport and Visa
Before the trip, I needed a passport and a visa. First, I went to the passport office with my father. We waited in a long queue for an hour. Then I prepared the visa documents: a letter from the university, my bank information and a photo. It was a lot of paperwork, but finally everything was ready.

# 5 | Bilet almak | Buying a Ticket
Next, I bought a plane ticket online. There were many flights from Ankara to London. Some flights were cheap, but they stopped in another city. I chose a direct flight. It was more expensive, but it was faster and easier. The flight was on the first of July, at 6:00 in the morning. I printed the ticket and put it in my bag.

# 6 | Bavul hazırlamak | Packing
The night before the trip, I packed my suitcase. My mother helped me. She said: "London is often rainy, even in summer. Take a raincoat!" I packed clothes for four weeks, a pair of comfortable shoes, my laptop, a dictionary and some Turkish tea for my host family. My suitcase was very heavy. I could not close it!

# 7 | Havalimanında | At the Airport
My family took me to the airport early in the morning. At the airport, I checked in and left my suitcase. Then I said goodbye to my family. My mother cried a little, and Ayşe hugged me. I walked through security and waited at the gate. I was nervous because it was my first flight.

# 8 | Uçuş | The Flight
The plane took off at 6:15. At first, I was afraid, but soon I relaxed. I sat next to the window. Below me, I saw clouds, mountains and the sea. The flight attendant brought breakfast and a cup of coffee. I watched a film and studied some English words. After four hours, the pilot said: "We are landing in London."

# 9 | Londra'ya varış | Arriving in London
At London Heathrow, I showed my passport and my visa. The officer asked: "What is the reason for your visit?" I answered: "I am here for an English course." He smiled and said: "Welcome to the United Kingdom." Then I took my suitcase and found the exit. It was grey and cloudy outside, just like my mother said.

# 10 | Ev sahibi aile | The Host Family
I stayed with a host family, the Wilsons. Mrs Wilson met me at the station. She was a friendly woman of about fifty. She had two children: Lucy, who was twelve, and Jack, who was eight. Their house was in a quiet street with small gardens. My room was upstairs. It was simple but comfortable. I gave them the Turkish tea, and they loved it.

# 11 | İlk akşam yemeği | The First Dinner
At seven o'clock, we had dinner together. Mrs Wilson cooked roast chicken with potatoes and carrots. Mr Wilson asked me many questions about Turkey. I tried to answer in English. Sometimes I did not know a word, so I used my hands. Everybody laughed, but it was a friendly laugh. After dinner, I was very tired, and I went to bed early.

# 12 | Kursun ilk günü | The First Day of the Course
The next morning, I took the underground to the language school. The school was in the centre of London, near a big park. There were students from many countries in my class. The teacher, Sarah, asked us to introduce ourselves. I was shy, but I said: "My name is Deniz. I am an engineering student from Turkey."

# 13 | Sınıf arkadaşlarım | My Classmates
I made two good friends in the first week. Maria was from Spain. She studied architecture, and she loved old buildings. Kenji was from Japan. He was a computer science student, and he was very good at games. We always sat together in class. At lunchtime, we ate sandwiches in the park and practised English.

# 14 | Metroda | On the Underground
The London underground is very old. It opened in 1863. People call it "the Tube". There are many lines, and each line has a different colour. On my first day, I took the wrong line and arrived late. After that, I used a map on my phone. I also bought a travel card. It was cheaper than buying a single ticket every day.

# 15 | Müze ziyareti | A Museum Visit
On Saturday, Maria, Kenji and I visited the Science Museum. It was free! I saw old steam engines, the first cars and even real space rockets. I spent two hours in the engineering hall. Maria preferred the history section, and Kenji played with the computer games. In the museum shop, I bought a small model of a steam engine for my brother.

# 16 | Şemsiye kayboldu | The Lost Umbrella
On Monday, it rained all day. I left my umbrella on the bus, so I got very wet. When I arrived at school, my clothes and my hair were wet. Sarah gave me a towel and a hot drink. "Welcome to London," she said, and she laughed. After class, I went to a shop and bought a new umbrella. This time, I was careful.

# 17 | İngiliz yemeği | British Food
Many people say that British food is not good, but I did not agree. I tried fish and chips, and it was delicious. On Sunday, the Wilsons made a traditional roast dinner. I also liked the pies and the cakes. However, I did not enjoy one thing: beans on toast for breakfast! I missed Turkish breakfast with cheese, olives and fresh bread.

# 18 | Kayboldum | Getting Lost
One evening, I wanted to walk home from the station. I thought I knew the way, but I turned into the wrong street. Soon all the streets looked the same. My phone battery was empty. I felt worried. Finally, I asked an old woman for help. She was very kind and walked with me to the end of the road. From there, I recognised our house.

# 19 | Pazar alışverişi | Shopping at the Market
On Sunday, I went to a famous market in Camden with Maria. There were hundreds of small shops and food stalls. People sold clothes, old records, jewellery and art. I bought a T-shirt with a picture of the Tube map. Maria bought earrings for her sister. The prices were not low, but we enjoyed looking at everything.

# 20 | Bir mektup | A Letter Home
Dear Mum and Dad, I am fine and happy. London is a big and exciting city. My English is getting better every day. The Wilsons are very nice to me. Yesterday I helped Mr Wilson in the garden, and Lucy taught me some English songs. I miss your cooking, though! Say hello to Ayşe and Mert. Love, Deniz

# 21 | Futbol maçı | A Football Match
Mr Wilson was a big football fan. One evening, he took me and Jack to a football match. The stadium was huge, and it was full of people. Everybody sang and shouted. Our team lost the first half, but then they scored two goals. In the end, they won 2–1. Jack jumped up and down with joy. It was a fantastic night.

# 22 | Kütüphanede | In the Library
In the second week, we had a project. Each group had to give a presentation about a famous British engineer. My group chose Isambard Kingdom Brunel. We went to a big public library to do research. Brunel built bridges, tunnels and ships in the nineteenth century. I was surprised that one person did so many things. We worked hard on our presentation.

# 23 | Sunum | The Presentation
On Friday, we gave our presentation. I spoke about Brunel's bridges, Maria spoke about his life, and Kenji showed the pictures. At the beginning, my hands were shaking, but then I felt more confident. When we finished, the other students clapped. Sarah said: "Very good work, and your pronunciation is much clearer now." I was proud of our group.

# 24 | Hasta oldum | I Got Ill
In the third week, I caught a cold. I had a sore throat and a high temperature. I stayed in bed for two days. Mrs Wilson was very kind. She made me soup and gave me some medicine. She also called the school to explain. Maria and Kenji sent me messages with the homework. On the third day, I felt much better.

# 25 | Cambridge gezisi | A Trip to Cambridge
At the weekend, our school organised a trip to Cambridge. We travelled by train. It took about one hour. Cambridge is a small city with a very old university. We visited some of the colleges and walked along the river. Then we went punting. That means you sit in a flat boat and a person pushes it with a long stick. It was relaxing and fun.

# 26 | Konser | A Concert
On Thursday evening, Kenji had two tickets for a concert in a park. A famous band was playing. We sat on the grass with thousands of other people. The music was loud and the lights were beautiful. I did not know the songs, but I danced anyway. We came home very late, so I tried to be quiet when I opened the door.

# 27 | Cüzdanım | My Wallet
One morning, I could not find my wallet. I looked everywhere: in my room, in my bag and in my jacket. My bank card and some money were inside. I was very worried. I called my bank and stopped the card. Then I went to the police station. Two days later, a man found it on the bus and gave it to the police. Nothing was missing!

# 28 | Ülkeleri karşılaştırmak | Comparing Countries
In class, we compared our countries. Maria said that Spain is sunnier and warmer than Britain. Kenji said that Tokyo is bigger than London, and the trains are faster. I said that Turkish people are very hospitable and that our food is the best in the world. Everybody laughed. We agreed that every country has good and bad sides.

# 29 | Gelecek planları | Future Plans
One evening, Maria, Kenji and I talked about our future. Maria wants to design museums. Kenji hopes to work for a game company in Tokyo. I said that I want to work on electric cars, maybe in another country first and then in Turkey. We promised to visit each other one day. I hope we keep our promise.

# 30 | İngilizcem gelişiyor | My English Is Improving
I noticed that my English was improving. In the first week, I needed to think for a long time before I spoke. Now, I can understand most conversations, and I can answer quickly. I still make mistakes, especially with grammar, but I am not afraid any more. Sarah says that the best way to learn is to use the language every day.

# 31 | Bir iş görüşmesi | A Job Interview
On the last Friday, Sarah organised a practice job interview. I had to apply for a job in an engineering company. I wore a shirt and a tie. The "manager" asked: "What are your strengths?" I said that I am hardworking and that I like solving problems. It was only practice, but I learned useful phrases for my future.

# 32 | Veda partisi | The Goodbye Party
On our last day, the school had a goodbye party. Every student brought food from their country. I made a big tray of börek. Maria brought tortilla, and Kenji made sushi. We ate, danced and took many photos. Sarah gave us our certificates. I felt happy and sad at the same time. I did not want to leave.

# 33 | Wilsonlara hediye | A Gift for the Wilsons
Before I left, I wanted to thank the Wilsons. I bought flowers for Mrs Wilson and a book about Istanbul for Mr Wilson. I gave Lucy and Jack Turkish delight. Mrs Wilson hugged me and said: "You are part of our family now. Please come back." Jack asked if he could visit me in Ankara. I said: "Of course!"

# 34 | Eve dönüş | Coming Home
The flight home was quiet. I looked out of the window and remembered everything: the Tube, the rain, the museum, my friends. When I arrived in Ankara, my whole family was waiting for me. Mert ran to me, and I gave him the model steam engine. That night, my mother cooked my favourite dishes. It was good to be home.

# 35 | Anlatacak çok şey | So Much to Tell
For days, I told everyone about London. I showed my photos to Can. He was a little jealous. "Next summer, I want to go too," he said. I told him about the language school and the course prices. I also gave him a tip: "Speak English all the time, even with other foreign students. That is the secret."

# 36 | İkinci yıl başlıyor | The Second Year Begins
In September, my second year at university started. This year, the classes were more difficult: mechanics, materials and computer programming. But I felt more confident. My English helped me a lot, because many of our books are in English. Now I can read technical texts without a dictionary, or almost without one.

# 37 | Bir kulübe katılmak | Joining a Club
I joined the university robotics club. The club meets every Wednesday in a small workshop. We build simple robots and take part in competitions. The members are friendly, and some of them are in their final year. They know a lot. In my first meeting, I learned how to use a soldering iron. I burned my finger, but only a little!

# 38 | İlk robotum | My First Robot
With two other members, I built my first robot. It was a small car that could follow a black line on the floor. We used two motors, a battery, some sensors and a cheap computer board. At first, the robot went in circles. We changed the program many times. Finally, it followed the line perfectly. We were delighted.

# 39 | Arkadaşlara e-posta | Emails to Friends
I often write emails to Maria and Kenji. Maria started a new project about old bridges in Seville. Kenji got a summer job at a game company, and he loved it. I told them about the robotics club and my robot. Writing to them helps me practise my English, and it keeps our friendship alive even though we are far away.

# 40 | Bir yıl sonra | One Year Later
A year ago, I could only say simple sentences in English. Now, I can travel alone, make friends, give a presentation and write emails. I know that I still have a lot to learn. Next, I want to use English in my studies and in my future job. My new goal is clear: an internship in an international engineering company.
